Min/max are read from the first row-group of one file and are shown for the first few columns only — they illustrate the statistics the engine prunes on, not the range across the whole dataset. A blank cell means that column carries no statistics in the footer.

Why partition columns are different

Partition columns are not stored inside the Parquet files at all — their values come from the directory names, which is why a predicate on one can eliminate whole files without opening any of them. Predicates on data columns have to fall back to footer statistics, which skip row-groups instead.
